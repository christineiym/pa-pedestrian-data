About this dashboard

This prototype shows whether machine-readable pedestrian accessibility data exists for each of Pennsylvania's 2,572 municipalities, and who publishes it. It measures data coverage, not whether the infrastructure itself is accessible.

Caveats

  • OpenStreetMap coverage levels ("partial" / "full") use uncalibrated placeholder thresholds; a sample audit in Pittsburgh and Philadelphia is planned.
  • Catalog datasets other than OSM and GTFS (PennDOT, DVRPC, city datasets) are unverified candidates and are excluded from scores unless you opt in.
  • The paratransit area is derived from the ADA minimum rule (¾ mile around non-commuter fixed bus routes and rail stations, 49 CFR 37.131) using GTFS. It is not any agency's official service area.
  • Transit data comes from GTFS feeds listed in the Mobility Database; some listed feeds are expired (flagged in the feeds table).

Sources and licenses
